# Environment art handoff

The [Buried Forest asset catalog](../../previews/buried-forest/README.md) contains the root arch render, GLB and Blender generator. Blender 4.3.2 imported the exported GLB with 12 meshes. Thirty-five sample rays confirmed clearance in the central test opening. Verify the final pawn clearance and set up collision in Unreal.

The floor 12 overlook render and GLB are also in the catalog. Blender imported the 16 meshes and 35 sample rays verified the level tread. Confirm clearance, collision and the connection to the overlook approach in Unreal.

The floor 13 ladder reimported as 43 meshes with separate groups for the moving ladder, fixed hoist and counterweight. Confirm orientation after importing the hinge axis in Unreal and calibrate the prop to the shortcut geometry.

The floor 14 handwheel has a separate moving pivot and fixed intake. Blender reimport and a quarter-turn confirmed the axis and the fixed collar position. Validate Unreal pivot conversion, grate collision and the water animation.

The floor 15 socket and pin GLB reimported as 21 meshes. Thirty samples along the extraction confirmed clearance between the keyed pin and fixed socket. The GLB includes the seated pose and pivot. Check scale, smoky glass material and attachment offsets in Unreal.

The floor 16 grate and stone counterweight have independent lifts with opposite 2.25 m travel. Blender verified the imported moving groups and central opening. Test clearance with the Unreal capsule, then animate the rope to the authored open and closed positions.

The floor 17 bridge has independent fixed and rotating groups. Blender accepted 19 sampled poses without intersection with the fixed islet. Fit a wider modular deck to the floor 17 banks before checking the runtime sweep.

The floor 18 latch has a local Y handle axis and a separate retractable tooth. Blender verified both seated poses and the travel between them with the tooth retracted. Calibrate the authored handle movement to the detents after import.
