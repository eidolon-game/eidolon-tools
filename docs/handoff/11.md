# Creature art handoff

The [Buried Forest asset catalog](../../previews/buried-forest/README.md) contains the Root Stalker render, GLB and Blender generator. Blender 4.3.2 imported the exported GLB with 161 meshes and 64,808 triangles. Stage the skeletal rig and animations before binding the asset to the encounter in Unreal.

Riftroot Tangle reimported as 59 meshes with separate limb and wedge pivots at the floor 20 design scale. Fit the rig, wedge lift, damage cavity and turn clearance before binding combat animation to the native encounter.
