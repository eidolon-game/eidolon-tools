# Audio handoff

The [Buried Forest asset catalog](../../previews/buried-forest/README.md) contains the original 22-second ambience and its source. Two runs of `python3 scripts/audio/render_buried_forest_ambience.py` with separate output directories produced identical WAVs. The loop is stereo PCM16 at 48 kHz. Configure looping and verify level, transitions and cue playback in Unreal.

The Fern Basin handwheel and drain release cues use mono PCM16 at 48 kHz. Independent regeneration matched the files and manifest. Bind the handwheel to a confirmed drain activation and the release to confirmed full drainage.

The Hollow Trunk pickup and seating cues use mono PCM16 at 48 kHz. Independent regeneration matched both files and the manifest. Bind the pickup cue to the actual grab and the seating cue to the saved checkpoint.

The Rootlet Canal chain and gate release cue is a 2.68-second mono PCM16 one-shot at 48 kHz. Regeneration matched its WAV and manifest. Bind it to a newly confirmed counterweight release and test audibility from the inside landing.

The Suspended Trunks rotation cue is a 2.58-second mono PCM16 one-shot at 48 kHz. Regeneration matched the WAV and manifest. Bind it to a newly confirmed alignment, with the final latch timed to the bridge movement.
